04 / APPROACHUnderstand it before embellishing it

Visible progress.
Fewer surprises.

Each engagement is scoped around the work itself. These are the steps I use to keep decisions clear and software maintainable.

  1. 01

    Get specific

    Name the user, the real job, the constraints, and the smallest useful finish line.

    ↘
  2. 02

    Make it tangible

    Work through the interaction and technical shape early, while it is still inexpensive to change.

    ↘
  3. 03

    Build in useful slices

    Keep each step reviewable, testable, and connected to the actual outcome.

    ↘
  4. 04

    Leave it operable

    Hand over the source, checks, decisions, and recovery notes that help the next person continue.

    ↘
